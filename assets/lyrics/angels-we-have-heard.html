Traditional French Carol

1. Angels we have heard on high sweetly singing o'er the plains.
And the mountains in reply echoing their joyous strains.

Refrain:
Gloria in excelsis Deo.
Gloria in excelsis Deo.

2. Shepherds, why this jubilee? Why your joyous strains prolongs?
Say what may the tidings be, which inspire your heaven'ly song.
Repeat Refrain

3. Come to Bethlehem and see Him whose birth the angels sing.
Come adore on bended knee, Christ the Lord, the newborn King.
Repeat Refrain
